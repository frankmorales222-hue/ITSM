# End-user guide

## What Northstar Desk is for

Northstar Desk is the company service desk for incidents, access requests, hardware and software help, questions, and change-related requests. Use one request for one issue so the service team can route, prioritize, and report on it accurately.

## Sign in

1. Open the Northstar Desk website.
2. Choose **Sign in with Microsoft** and complete your company sign-in and MFA prompt. Your company email identifies you automatically.
3. If Microsoft sign-in is unavailable, use the local account provided by IT. Use **Forgot password or first time signing in?** when you need a reset link or first-time activation.

## Submit a request

1. Select **Create Request**.
2. Choose the request form that best matches the need, such as IT support, incident, access, hardware, or change.
3. Enter a short, searchable summary. Include what you expected, what happened, when it started, and how many people are affected.
4. Select the affected device or service when the form offers it. Your known asset information may be filled automatically from the endpoint inventory.
5. Select urgency and impact honestly. Use high priority only when work is blocked, a security concern exists, or multiple people are affected.
6. Add screenshots or approved diagnostic files. Never upload passwords, MFA codes, payment information, medical information, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive data.
7. Review the form and submit. Save a draft if you need more information before sending.

## Follow your requests

**My Requests** shows open, waiting, resolved, and closed work. Open a request to read its public timeline, current status, assigned team, and technician replies. Internal IT notes and restricted notes are never shown to requesters.

Keep the ticket number in the subject when replying by email. Replies from the website or your email client stay in the same ticket when the original message headers and ticket number are preserved.

## Reply, attach, or use chat

- Use **Reply** in the ticket to ask a follow-up question or provide requested details.
- Reply to the service-desk email instead of starting a new message when possible.
- Use live chat when it is offered for the ticket; the conversation is retained with the request.
- Do not send credentials or confidential information through chat or email.

## Resolution and closure

When IT marks a request **Resolved**, test the result. Confirm that the issue is fixed or reply with what remains broken. If help is still needed, reopen the request within the configured reopen period (normally seven days) and explain what you tried.

If you reply that the issue is resolved, the system may close the request automatically according to the service-desk policy and record that you requested closure.

## Suggested solutions

For approved low-risk issues, IT may send safe troubleshooting steps in the ticket and by email. Select **Yes, close my ticket**, **No, I still need help**, or **Request live help**. In email, place exactly `FIXED`, `IT WORKED`, `DID NOT WORK`, or `REQUEST LIVE HELP` on the first non-empty line. Your response and the original guidance remain part of the same ticket.

## Asset and agent support

If the Northstar endpoint agent is installed, it reports device information such as hostname, operating system, hardware, security status, and installed software to the service desk. The agent does not store ticket content locally. Use its tray menu to open Northstar Desk, create a request, or see updates when those options are enabled by IT.

## Service announcements and help

Read active announcements on the home page before submitting a duplicate request. If the site is unavailable, contact your service desk through the alternate channel provided by your company and include your name, email, device hostname, and a brief description of the issue.
