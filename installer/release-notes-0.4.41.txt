Northstar Desk 0.4.41

- Added tenant-safe Smart Self-Service using administrator-approved troubleshooting articles.
- Added configurable confidence, safety exclusions, technician-suggestion mode, and approved automatic delivery.
- Added requester outcomes for fixed, not fixed, and live-help escalation while preserving the ticket timeline.
- Added knowledge article draft, review, publishing, version history, usage metrics, and audit events.
- Added Smart Self-Service controls in Help Desk Settings and suggestion status on Ticket Detail.
- Restricted ticket message visibility by role so requester views cannot expose internal or restricted notes.
- Smart Self-Service is disabled by default and does not change existing ticket handling until an administrator enables it.
