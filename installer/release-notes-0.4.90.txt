Email intake reliability release: an inbound email that hit an error mid-processing could create a duplicate ticket on the next mailbox poll; each message is now saved or rolled back on its own and retried at most 3 times before going to manual review. Notification emails are no longer re-sent when a later worker step fails. The mailbox poll skips already-processed messages before downloading them, greatly reducing Microsoft Graph calls. Replies with an unpadded ticket number (e.g. INC-123) now thread correctly. No database schema changes. Reply-matching rules, sender authorization, and ticket routing are unchanged.
